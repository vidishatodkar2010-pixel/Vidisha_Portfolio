Fintech · RuPay

Taking on a 20-Year Duopoly

Content IP strategy across IPL & festive platforms

+15%Market Share
+6%Brand Power
+9%Recommendation

Challenge

RuPay held ~12% value share against Visa & Mastercard's combined 75–80%. Competing feature-for-feature was structurally unwinnable.

Strategic Thinking

Young India treats spontaneity as identity. A domestic network couldn't out-spec incumbents, but could own cultural proximity — living inside IPL and festive moments this audience organizes their year around.

Approach

  • Built "Celebrating Spontaneity" brand platform
  • Sequenced 3-year content IP roadmap across IPL & festive windows
  • Extended into NCMC transit payments as proof of the spontaneity promise

Overtook Visa & Mastercard on consideration, preference, and recommendation by changing the competitive axis from features to cultural relevance.

Key TakeawayAgainst a legacy duopoly, change what's being judged — legacy stops being an advantage.

FMCG · Johnson's Baby

Project Golden Minute

Proving a heritage promise instead of restating it

200K+Midwives
+4.3%Ad Recall
6.3MYouTube

Challenge

From monopoly to fragmented market. Consumers rated the brand high on trust but low on innovation. Modern mothers had lost emotional connection.

Strategic Thinking

Tied "gentleness" to the first sixty seconds after birth, where neonatal revival knowledge among rural Dai Maas decides a newborn's survival.

Results

Ad Recall +4.3% (1.6× benchmark) · Consideration +2.7% (4× benchmark) · Purchase Intent +1.3% (2.1× benchmark) · 3M+ organic views

◆ Cannes Lions Shortlist 2026◆ 6 Kyoorius Elephants◆ Abby Award

Key TakeawayThe fastest way back to relevance is proving your oldest promise in a new, high-stakes context.

FMCG · Navratna

Legacy Brand Meets Gen Z

Reframing cooling oil as a stress-reset ritual

15+Strategies
50%Pitch Win
2Nominations

Challenge

Grow a 15–25 youth audience without diluting trust the core older user base was built on.

Strategic Thinking

Don't reposition the product — reposition the job. Projective research surfaced youth stressors; the cooling effect was reframed as an everyday mental reset.

Key TakeawayAudience extension rarely needs a new truth — just the job the audience already wants it to do.

Making a category leader worth advocating for again

25+Interviews
6Territories

Challenge

Netflix needed long-term brand affinity. Non-members felt it was distant, too edgy, expensive, and not for families.

Strategic Thinking

Recommendation is the real currency. The brand's job shifted from "convince" to "give something worth putting social capital behind."

CMO/MD-level buy-in. Client leadership called it "fresh and hard-hitting".
